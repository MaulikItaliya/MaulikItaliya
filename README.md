## Featured Projects

### [Mixed-Signal Embedded Control Board (STM32F407)](https://github.com/MaulikItaliya/Mixed-Signal-Board)
4-layer mixed-signal board designed in Altium Designer, with full schematic and design documentation.
- **Interfaces:** 10/100 Ethernet (DP83826, MII), USB-UART (CH340C), on-board STM32F103 JTAG/SWD debugger
- **Motor control:** 2× DRV8701 drivers with N-MOSFET H-bridges, 36 W per channel
- **Analog and audio:** ADS122C04 24-bit load-cell ADC, CS43L22 stereo DAC, digital MEMS microphone
- **PCB:** solid GND plane, segmented power plane, mixed-signal partitioning

### ESP32-C3 Development Board
4-layer PCB with USB Type-C, RF layout and power management. Hand-assembled and tested.

### Arduino Uno-Style Development Board
ATmega328P board with 5 V and 3.3 V supplies and a USB programming interface. Complete schematic and PCB layout, hand-assembled.

### LTspice Circuit Simulations
DC-DC converter design, analog circuits, filters and power electronics.
